# Code of conduct

LogDelta is a small project maintained in spare time. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull
requests, discussions, reviews or anywhere else the project is represented) is expected to help
keep it a friendly and useful place.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** People come with different experience, languages and amounts of time.
  Assume good intent, and explain rather than dismiss.
- **Stay on the work.** Criticise code, ideas and decisions, not people. Disagreement is fine;
  make your case with examples and accept the outcome gracefully.
- **Respect people's time.** Search before opening an issue, include what is needed to reproduce
  a problem, and keep threads on topic.
- **Respect privacy.** Anonymise logs before you share them. Never post someone else's personal
  data, credentials or private messages.
- **Give credit.** Acknowledge other people's work and ideas.

## What is not acceptable

- harassment, insults, threats, or deliberate intimidation, in public or in private;
- discriminatory jokes or language, or unwelcome sexual attention or content;
- personal or political attacks, and trolling or repeated derailing of discussions;
- publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission;
- encouraging any of the above.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, report it privately: use **Report content** in
the `…` menu of the issue, pull request or comment, and choose to report it to the repository's
maintainers. For anything that does not fit there, or for behaviour outside GitHub, open a
private report from the repository's **Security** tab and mark it as a conduct report.

Reports are read only by the maintainer and handled confidentially. You will get an answer, and
you will not face retaliation for reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this
code, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful. The
response depends on how serious the behaviour is and whether it continues after a warning. A
single severe incident (such as threats or doxxing) can lead to an immediate ban.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and in public spaces when someone is representing the
project, for example when posting as a maintainer or on the project's behalf.
